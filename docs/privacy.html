Metronome Perfect

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Metronome Perfect collects no data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (tempo, time signature, chosen sound and colour palette) are saved on your device so the app opens the way you left it.
  • Sounds you record are saved on your device, inside the app. They are included in your device's own backups, like any other app data, but are never sent to me or anyone else.

The microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you choose to record your own click sound, and it listens only while you're recording. Nothing is uploaded.

Sharing a sound

If you tap the share button on one of your sounds, the app creates a small .metrosound file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it (for example Messages or AirDrop). Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete a recorded sound by holding or double-tapping it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
