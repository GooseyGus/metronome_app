I can't hear the click

Turn the volume up with the side buttons. The click plays even with the silent switch on. If you're on a phone call, the metronome stops and you'll need to press START again afterwards.

Can it play over my music?

Yes. The click mixes over music and video from other apps without stopping them, and keeps going with the screen locked.

How do I set a time signature?

Tap either number of the time signature to type it in. Turning the time signature on accents the first beat of each bar, and the click follows the note value, so 6/8 clicks eighth notes.

How do I record my own sound?

Swipe the row of sounds to the end and tap +. Clap, click or say "hey!" for the beat, and optionally record a different downbeat. Hold or double-tap a sound to edit, share or delete it.

How do I change the colours?

Tap the palette button in the top right to cycle through the eight palettes.
